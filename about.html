THE PERSON BEHIND THE SCOOP.

It started with
one very
good dog.

Ayden started Poop’em Scoop’em out of love for his own dog. What began as something deeply personal became a way to care for other pets—and the people who share their homes.

That’s still what drives the work. Showing up, taking care of the small things, and leaving your space better than we found it.

When Ayden started the company in an attempt to save his dog’s life, he saw the opportunity to make caring for pets his full-time work. Today, Poop’em Scoop’em offers pet waste removal, yard cleanup, feeding and litter services, with flexible scheduling and plans shaped around each home.
